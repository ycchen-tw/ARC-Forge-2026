# DC22 — Click panels to reshape terrain, drag bridges

Keywords: click-to-toggle terrain, rotating bridge, sliding platform, portal pair, teleport by standing on pad, hidden button unlocked by pickup, pressure plate, remote-controlled grabber, drag a bridge, fall penalty, move budget, 2-cell grid steps

Looks like: A 64-wide board with a left puzzle area on dark background and a right panel of colored button icons, split by a dashed white divider. A 2×2 green block must reach a 2×2 yellow block. Actions are four direction moves (2 cells each) plus click, with no undo. Floor is solid colored tiles. The icons in the right panel are buttons that reshape tiles on the left. A bottom-row bar shows the budget.

## Rules
- The player is a 2×2 block and moves 2 cells per direction action. It stands only on "floor", checked at its upper-left cell. Checkerboard tiles, background and all-white tiles are not floor. Landing exactly on the goal clears the level.
- Each panel button toggles or advances every tile in its color group at once. Forms include a 90° rotation, a 2-form solid/checker swap, and a multi-phase bridge or platform that slides 2 cells per click. A group move never carries the player.
- Clicking a panel costs 2 moves and a blank click costs 1. If a click leaves no floor under the player, the scene rolls back and 20 moves are lost, shown as a 15-frame wipe. Standing on a hub or pivot that maps to itself is safe.
- Clicking tiles in the playfield does nothing. Some buttons start hidden: walking onto a small icon in a box (overlap suffices) makes that button appear and clickable. Keys and pressure plates work the same way.
- Portals are 2×2 checker pairs. Clicking their button while the player stands exactly on one jumps to the partner of the same color pair. A selector button can cycle a portal's color pair, which changes its destination.
- From level 5 a grabber on a track moves via 4 direction buttons plus a grab button. Grabbing is permanent, so the carried bridge then moves 4 cells per press. In level 6 the direction buttons only appear while the player stands on matching floor plates.
- Budget is 128/192/192/192/512/1024 moves by level. The last level's goal sits across the divider, in the right half.

## Levels
- L1: two buttons. A rotating bridge and a solid/checker swap; ride the bridge by toggling after you cross.
- L2: a rotating bar rotates about a hub. The red button is hidden and unlocked by walking onto an icon in a sealed box.
- L3: portal pair (cleared 15/15, median 36 min). Several tile groups flip together; a plate unlocks the purple button.
- L4: sliding platforms plus iris-bridge buttons. The two bridges are always 4 phases apart, so they never connect at the same time, and both are disconnected in six of the eight phases (cleared 14/15, median 36 min).
- L5: a track-mounted grabber drags a bridge (hard: cleared 10/14, median 163 min, 4 stuck).
- L6: plate-gated grabber, color-cycled portal, goal across the divider (hard: cleared 4/10, median 154 min, 6 stuck).

## Blind spots — where our player lost time
1. **Believed:** In L6 the goal room is sealed and nothing can reach it, so a mechanic is missing. The red "grab" button looked like a pulse with no effect (6 copies). **Actually:** Grabbing works only when the ring's hole sits exactly over the bar's pivot; it then drags the pivot, and the bar with it. Gripping the bar's end does nothing. **Cue:** A 6-frame pulse with 0 net change means "refused", not "inert". **Test:** Move the ring until its center coincides with the pivot, press grab, then press one direction and see whether the bar shifts.
2. **Believed:** In L5 the red button is a decoy and the start pocket is sealed (3 copies). **Actually:** The grab works only at one track position, where the claw mouth holds the bar's nub. Afterwards the bar follows the claw as a movable bridge. **Cue:** The pocket has a gap exactly the bar's width. **Test:** Try the grab at every reachable claw position, then move once.
3. **Believed:** The player block was trapped in its start box, so only toggle combinations mattered (6 copies, up to 101 min in L1). **Actually:** It walks off the box onto any floor tile that becomes solid. **Cue:** The exits were blocked only by checker tiles. **Test:** Toggle the nearest checker to solid and step toward it.
4. **Believed:** Riding a moving platform or hub carries the player (7 copies). **Actually:** The player stays put, and a click that leaves it with no floor is rolled back with a penalty. A direction move toward a floorless cell just leaves it in place, with no penalty. **Cue:** A 15-frame wipe. **Test:** Click while standing at the platform's far end and see whether you stay covered; alternate click and step to "surf".
5. **Believed:** A small button icon in a box is clickable, or the missing button simply doesn't exist (5 copies). **Actually:** You must walk onto it. **Cue:** A tiny copy of a panel button on a pad. **Test:** Step onto the icon, then check the panel for a new button.
6. **Believed:** The portal button is cosmetic when nothing moves (4 copies). **Actually:** It teleports only if the player stands exactly on a portal tile. Without that it just flips the tiles' phase. **Cue:** An 8-cell change. **Test:** Stand on one 2×2 checker and click its button.
7. **Believed:** ACTION2 means up (6 copies). **Actually:** ACTION1 is up and ACTION2 is down. **Test:** Send one move and watch.

## Breakthroughs
- L6: align the ring over the pivot, grab, and drag the pivot along the track. Then teleport away and rotate the bar back, which lays it as a bridge. Finally walk across and hop into the goal room along a row that crosses the dashed divider. 4 copies.
- L5: grab at the one aligned spot, then park the dragged bar as a ladder or bridge between isolated platforms.
- Walk onto hidden icons, and stand on portals before clicking.

## Lessons that carry to other games
- A "failed" action that animates (pulse or shake) with no net change may be a precondition not met. Look for an alignment condition before calling it dead.
- Before ruling a region unreachable, ask what object could be moved to bridge it, and test grabbing it at every position.
- An effect that is invisible when nothing is selected may need the player to stand on a target. Test the action from on top of each candidate object.
- A control may do nothing until its own unlock condition is met. Look for hidden items to pick up.
- A moving surface may not carry you. Check whether you stay covered afterwards.
- Verify the direction mapping with one move before running a batch.
