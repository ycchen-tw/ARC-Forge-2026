# WA30 — Grab-and-drag boxes into zones, with autonomous helper and thief robots
Keywords: grab and carry, fixed carry offset, Sokoban-like, autonomous helper agent, thief agent, decoy zone, walls that block the player but not carried objects, hand-off through a barrier, action budget bar, dispel enemy by facing, sealed regions, last-box ordering
Looks like: A 64×64 board of 4×4 cells with a green block that moves one cell per ACTION1–4 (up, down, left, right). ACTION5 grabs or releases the thing in the faced cell. There are dark blocks with a blue core, a blue-bordered gray "zone", and a pink bar along the bottom edge that counts down. Later boards add orange and purple wandering blocks, black or hatched walls, and plain gray patches that look like zones but have no blue border.

## Rules
- The player has a facing. A blocked move still turns the player when empty-handed, and that costs an action. ACTION5 acts only on the faced cell. It grabs a box (even one a robot is holding), releases a held box, or deletes a thief robot.
- A held box keeps the offset it had at grab time, and facing is locked while holding. The box moves rigidly with the player (push, pull or slide), and a move fails if either the player's or the box's destination is blocked. The player can never push an ungrabbed box.
- Win: every box is inside a bordered target zone and nobody is holding any box. A box in the player's hand, or still in a robot's hand on the tick it lands, does not count. Surplus zone cells may stay empty. Plain solid-gray patches without the border are decoys.
- The bar is the action budget, and its drain rate differs per level (roughly 70 to 200 actions). At zero the game is over and only RESET works.
- Wall-like tiles block a walking actor but not a carried box. A box may rest on or slide through hatched cells, but not through solid black cells.
- Orange helpers fetch the nearest free box and carry it at a fixed offset. They drop it on a free zone cell only if they can stand on (slot − offset). They freeze if no reachable box or slot exists. Helpers and thieves act after every player action, including no-ops and blocked moves. Moving first, then thieves, and a robot's grab ends its type's update for that tick.
- Purple thieves take boxes, including ones already on target cells, and dump them on a decoy patch. Face one and press ACTION5 to delete it. While targeted, its border turns yellow and the core stays purple. It can be dispelled while it carries something, and the box it holds drops in place.

## Levels
- L1: three boxes into a 3-cell zone; the last one must be released.
- L2 (hard: cleared 14/14, median 88 min): first orange helper. 5 boxes, 6 slots, 70 actions, so the helper's work must run in parallel.
- L3 (hard: cleared 12/14, median 41 min, 1 stuck): a full-height wall splits the board. The player is stuck on the left, and the zone and the helper are on the right.
- L4: the player starts sealed in a hatched room with 3 helpers in separate regions and 5 small zones (7 cells in total).
- L5 (hard: cleared 9/11, median 70 min, 2 stuck): a black wall with a 2-lane corridor. The player and all boxes start on one side, and the zone and the helper on the other.
- L6: first purple thief and decoy patch. A wall has a 1-row gap, so carry horizontally.
- L7: corridor with the thief and a decoy patch.
- L8 (hard: cleared 5/8, median 124 min, 1 stuck): 13 boxes, 2 helpers, 2 thieves, 3 bands joined by gaps.
- L9 (hard: cleared 1/5, median 147 min, 4 stuck): about 70 actions, 9 boxes, a hatched band, a sealed helper, and a thief that exits a maze late on a fixed schedule.

## Blind spots — where our player lost time
1. **Dealing with the thief on L8/L9.**
   **Believed:** a thief can be chased, blocked, or fetched from deep in a maze, or just out-delivered.
   **Actually:** it is caught only when it sits in the faced cell, the player is empty-handed, and it is at a spot it will pass or park at. Standing in its path too early stalls it far away. The L9 thief arrives at a fixed time at a fixed exit.
   **Cue:** boxes already on target keep disappearing to the decoy patch, and chasing at equal speed makes it mirror sideways.
   **Test:** bump toward it (this sets facing), check for the yellow border, then ACTION5. Wait at a cell one step outside its chokepoint.
2. **Helper hand-off geometry and jams (L2, L5, L8, 4 copies).**
   **Believed:** a helper will place any box on any free slot; a box on a slot is finished.
   **Actually:** the helper needs the cell (slot − offset) free. Corner and inner slots become unfillable once neighbours fill, and helpers park in corridors.
   **Cue:** a helper stands still while carrying and free slots remain.
   **Test:** before the last boxes, list which side each carrier holds its box on, fill corner slots first, and shift a box yourself if needed.
3. **Handing a box to a robot that cannot reach a free slot (L4, 5 copies).**
   **Believed:** any wall cell is a fine drop point.
   **Actually:** each robot is locked to a region, and a frozen carrying robot means RESET.
   **Cue:** the robot never moves after grabbing.
   **Test:** flood-fill regions first. If it happens, face the carried box and press ACTION5 to take it back.
4. **Treating the dotted barrier as a hard stop (L3, 4 copies stuck or slow).**
   **Believed:** the player cannot move boxes across it, or a carried box cannot go farther than the player.
   **Actually:** a carried box slides ahead of the player onto forbidden tiles (grey, dotted) (grab from the west, walk east), and the helper extracts it.
   **Cue:** boxes already sit inside the barrier at the start.
   **Test:** grab a box from the side facing the barrier and walk toward it.
5. **Idling to "watch" the helper (L2, 4 copies).**
   **Believed:** observe the helper first, since moves are free.
   **Actually:** the bar is the real limit, and idling burned a whole attempt.
   **Cue:** the bar drains on no-ops.
   **Test:** act from move one, and split work so the helper and player run in parallel.
6. **Win condition misreads (L1, L2, 5 copies).**
   **Believed:** all slots covered, or a box in hand or a robot standing there counts.
   **Actually:** all boxes must be released and settled; surplus slots can stay empty.
   **Cue:** the level does not clear until a final ACTION5 or one more tick.
   **Test:** release the last box and take one extra no-op.
7. **Mixing up direction keys (L5, 2 copies).**
   **Believed:** ACTION2 and ACTION1 meant the opposite of what they do.
   **Cue:** a move that goes the wrong way.
   **Test:** make one probe move per level.

## Breakthroughs
- Push carried boxes onto forbidden tiles (grey, dotted) so the other side's robot can pick them up (L3, L4, L9 band).
- Delete thieves with ACTION5 after setting facing by bumping them; with the thief deleted, hand-carry the decoy-patch boxes back.
- Steal a stuck robot's box with ACTION5 and re-place it where another robot can reach it.
- Fill corner or hard-to-reach slots first, and shift boxes so carriers' offsets fit (L8).
- Split work: the player carries the far or awkward boxes, the helper takes the near ones (L2).

## Lessons that carry to other games
- If an obstacle blocks you, check whether it blocks what you carry; carried objects may follow different rules than the carrier.
- A helper's unseen constraints (reach, geometry, fixed offset) decide whether the last item can be placed. Plan the final items first.
- A "move failed" does not mean "action wasted": it can still turn you or advance the opponents. Test with a bump.
- Remove or neutralise a thief before depending on gains it can undo, and find where it will be rather than chasing it.
- When an NPC freezes, ask what it needs (reachable target, free standing cell) and whether you can grab its cargo.
- Distinguish look-alike goal areas by border and test with one drop.
- Check the budget rate on every new level before spending actions on observation.
