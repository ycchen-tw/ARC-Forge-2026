## Level {level} is dragging on

You have played level {level} across {windows} context windows and {actions} actions. When a level drags on like this, the usual cause is a belief in your notes that is wrong: an explanation inferred from what you saw, written down as if it had been seen, and stated more firmly each time the notes were rewritten -- while the steps it was fitted to fit another explanation just as well. A search or simulator that says something is impossible is only as right as its code.

Before anything else in this window:
1. List the beliefs that decide what you think is possible on this level -- what blocks what, how far things move, what ends the level, what your code ruled out -- and for each, whether you saw it hold in a test whose result you predicted beforehand, or inferred it: fitted to earlier steps after the fact, brought from an earlier level, or concluded by your code.
2. For each inferred belief, the cheapest test in actions that could show it wrong, and what each outcome would mean. Check a conclusion of your code by hand, or on a case whose answer you know (a level you cleared).
3. Run the cheapest of those tests first -- before more analysis of old steps and before more work on your simulator. A test of a few actions costs far less than a context window of analysis.

Move what you inferred out of Confirmed rules into Hypotheses, each with its test, and correct whatever the test shows.
