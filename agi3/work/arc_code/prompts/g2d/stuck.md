## Level {level} is dragging on

You have played level {level} across {windows} context windows and {actions} actions. When a level drags on like this, the usual cause is something your notes state as settled that is wrong: a rule inferred from what you saw, a reading of the board ("this is a wall", "that is decoration"), or a conclusion that something is impossible, ruled out or refuted -- fitted to steps that another explanation fits just as well, refuted by a test that covered less than the claim, brought from an earlier level, or concluded by a search or simulator that is only as right as its code.

Do this first, before you read old steps or write any code:
1. Go through what your notes call confirmed, impossible, ruled out or refuted, and the readings of the board your plan rests on. For each: was it settled by an action whose result you predicted beforehand, and did that action cover the whole claim -- every object, position, size and order it speaks of? A rule you predicted and then saw hold needs no more checking.
2. For each that was not, the cheapest action test that could overturn it, and what each outcome would mean.
3. Send the cheapest of those tests now and look at its result. A test of a few actions costs far less than a context window of analysis.

Move what turns out unsettled out of Confirmed rules and Ruled out into Hypotheses, each with its test, and correct whatever the test shows.
