Coordinates are always `(x, y)`: x is column, y is row, with the origin at the upper left.

**Core rules**: Control a square character through a maze and **collide with the exit** (a square door with color-9 border and color-10 interior, `exydhv`) to clear the level. Walking alone is insufficient: each level blocks part of the route with “too narrow,” “no passage,” or “a door,” requiring the **spell system** at the bottom (or upper left). Spells are not cast with a key: click the correct pattern on the **3×3 rune panel**, and it casts automatically once complete.

The screen always contains three UI components. **All three are solid obstacles that really block the character** (movement uses engine `try_move`, checking pixel collisions with **every** `collidable` sprite, including UI):
- **Rune panel**: A 17×17 panel at `(22,47)`–`(38,63)` contains nine clickable 3×3 cells in a 3×3 arrangement, with cell upper-left corners at `(24|29|34, 49|54|59)`. Nine `clcbko` sensor regions lie beneath it, with `visible=False, collidable=True`: **invisible but collidable**, making the whole 17×17 panel a wall to the character. **Official 0.9.3 allows clicking the sensor regions**, without hitting visible cells: x segments 22–27/28–32/33–38 and y segments 47–52/53–57/58–63 correspond to the three columns/rows. For example, `(22,47)` and `(25,50)` both toggle the upper-left cell; `(38,63)` toggles the lower-right cell.
- **Spellbook**: Solid, collidable 10×10 frames with color-3 border and color-2 interior (`sptivk-caxiiu`). Each frame holds one spell icon: **the number of frames equals the number of spells unlocked for this level**. The icon is the rune pattern required for that spell (each lit cell is 2×2, separated by 1 px). In levels 1–3 the frame sits left of the rune panel at `(11,50)`; in levels 4–6 frames move to the upper left at `(0,0)`/`(0,11)`/`(0,22)` (level 4 has only the first two).
- **Budget bar**: The rightmost two columns (x=62,63), 32 cells. All white (color 0) means charges have reached the limit, not necessarily a loss; the next charge exceeding it loses. It is also collidable, so x≥62 is inaccessible.

**Actions**
- `ACTION1/2/3/4` = up/down/left/right. The character **moves by its own body length**: 4 cells when 4×4, 2 when 2×2. If a 4-cell move is blocked, it automatically tries 2 cells instead (so the large body can adjust to an odd alignment after hitting a wall). Hitting a wall leaves it stationary but **still charges one step**. A directional input also turns the character (the color-9 side is its front); this determines fireball direction.
- `ACTION6(x,y)`:
  - Click a **rune cell** → toggle it on/off (lit cells can be cleared), **costing one step**.
  - Click a **spellbook icon or its frame** → select that spell as a hint; the rune panel shows its required cells in color 0 (white). Click the same one again → play a cell-by-cell flashing demonstration. **Free**.
  - Click **anywhere else, including blank space** → the entire rune panel flashes red (color 8), a “click here” hint. **Free**.
- Neither `ACTION5` nor `ACTION7` (Undo) is available.

**Three spells** (`zzpoabuniyn`, [row][column] corresponds to the rune panel's 3×3)

| Spell | Rune pattern | Suggested click coordinates | Color | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Resize | `.X.` / `X.X` / `.X.` (diamond, 4 cells) | (30,50) (25,55) (35,55) (30,60) | 15 | Toggle the body between **4×4 ↔ 2×2** |
| Teleport | `XX.` / `.X.` / `...` (upper-left shape, 3 cells) | (25,50) (30,50) (30,55) | 11 | Teleport to a **destination** (below) |
| Fireball | `.X.` / `.X.` / `.X.` (middle column, 3 cells) | (30,50) (30,55) (30,60) | 6 | Fire a ball in the current facing direction |

Patterns require an **exact match**: one extra or missing cell invalidates them. A matched spell must also be in the **current level's unlocked list**; otherwise it is treated as no pattern (cells stay lit in color 14 and must be cleared manually). `efvw` **directly overwrites this list each level; unlocks do not accumulate**: level 2 cannot resize, level 3 cannot teleport or resize, and level 4 cannot teleport. After a spell finishes, all nine cells clear automatically.

**Spell step costs**: Each pattern click costs 1 step, and automatic casting on completion **costs 1 additional step**. Thus fireball/teleport each cost 4, resize 5. (The number of externally submitted actions is 1 lower because casting finishes inside the same action as the final click. Level 2's shortest solution is 3 clicks + 2 movement inputs = 5 actions, matching its old human baseline of 5; the current baseline is 6.)

**Other mechanisms**

- **Size determines passage and firing lines**. The default body is 4×4, shrinking to 2×2. Consecutive normal large moves preserve the current position's remainder modulo 4; **coordinates need not be multiples of 4**. The 4×4 character in level 1 can occupy x=39/35/31/27/23; level 4 starts at y=19/23/27/31…. Wall-induced 2-cell moves, repositioning during enlargement, and teleportation can change this alignment; the starting remainder is not a level-wide invariant. The verified level-4 route first shrinks, moves to y=29, then fires left into the narrow passage at y=29,30.
- **The fireball ray always follows the row/column of the character's upper-left corner**. Firing right/down changes only the **starting position** to the body's right/bottom edge, not the row/column itself (so a downward ray also follows the character's leftmost column). Only three things block the ray: maze walls (color-5 `duvwsv-*`), closed doors, and switches. Hitting a **switch** removes it and **all corresponding doors**. Hitting a wall or door does nothing (wasting 4 steps).
- **Switches and doors are paired by color**: `tagsmh` (color-6 border, **color-13 center**) pairs with the color-13 maroon door `dosorb`; `seofsw-tagsmh` (**color-12 center**) pairs with the color-12 orange door. Both pairs occur in levels 5 and 6 and must be shot separately. Firing removes switch and corresponding door sprites; other objects remain, so traversable space must be reevaluated against remaining collisions.
- **Very dark gray constriction (color 4, `sprite-21`)**: Narrows a passage to 2 cells wide/high; **fireballs ignore it completely**. Local reachability checks in the old version/reconstructed engine found that even the small character could not pass because its alignment was off by one cell. All reachable states of the current version were not exhaustively checked, so this cannot be rewritten as a special engine rule forbidding every character from crossing.
- **Teleport destinations depend on body size**, and **a 6×6 corner ring marks the next destination onscreen** (both sizes are centered inside: large 4×4 destination = ring +1, small 2×2 = ring +2):
  - At 4×4 → land on color-11 large destination `tevyeq-tagsmh` (4×4 corner frame, **noncollidable**); color-11 indicator ring `acyylh-tevyeq-inkpfx` is visible only while 4×4.
  - At 2×2 → land on color-15 small destination `smzaik-tevyeq-tagsmh` (solid 2×2); color-15 ring `smzaik-tevyeq-inkpfx` is visible only while 2×2.
  - Multiple destinations **cycle in order** (level 6 has two large destinations); the ring always marks the **next** one.
  - Teleporting directly changes character coordinates, **without movement collisions, exit triggering, or energy pickup collection**. Teleporting next to a door is not equivalent to walking into the exit.
  - **The two indicator rings are mutually exclusive**: shrinking to 2×2 hides the large-destination ring, so **the next large teleport destination is unreadable onscreen** while small (this matters only for level 6's two large destinations). Resizing does not reset the cycling index.
  - A small destination additionally has a **4×4 color-11 corner frame** around it (level 5 `sprite-23`, level 6 `sprite-22`), visually identical to large destination `tevyeq-tagsmh`, but **collidable**, with its corners often overlapping walls. Distinguish it by position: it always exactly surrounds the small destination (small destination −1).
- **Enlarging needs space**: To grow from 2×2 to 4×4, the engine searches positions “here, or up to 2 cells left/up” for space to fit 4×4 (outer loop dx = −2,−1,0; inner dy = −2,−1,0, so upper left is tried first). If none fits, resizing fails: the character flashes color 4 and stays 2×2, but the full 5 steps are charged. Shrinking always succeeds without changing position.
  **This fit test differs from movement collision tests**: `qbdokwllfg` checks only maze walls `duvwsv-*`, `crzdcq`, and both door types. **Constrictions, switches, exits, and UI do not count as obstacles** (going outside 64×64 does). Thus enlargement can grow directly into constriction or exit cells.
- **Energy pickups (color-14 2×2, `enjehv-pahtoz`)**: Touch one to remove it and **refund 10 steps**. During movement, collection uses the character's **unscaled 2×2 box** (only the upper-left 2×2 counts when 4×4); after resizing it uses the full body box. Level 6's two pickups are **hidden beneath the two switches**, exposed only when fireballs remove them.
- **The exit triggers only when it blocks a movement attempt**: actively walk into it. That attempt is free; the character slides toward the exit **twice, half a body length each time** (4 cells total when 4×4, 2 when 2×2), then advances to the next level.
- **The first non-RESET action in level 1 is consumed**: it only plays the resize-pattern demonstration, without moving, toggling cells, or charging steps. Levels 1–3 automatically select their sole spell as a hint. Levels 4–6 do not automatically select a new hint, but may retain the previous level's hint field (next paragraph).
- **Fireball direction carries across levels**: `jdmucabyqar` is initialized to “down” only when the game is constructed, and is not reset on level changes (the character sprite's drawn direction comes from level data). Pressing a direction before firing is therefore safer.
- **Charging and outcome order**: Normal movement first attempts to move and collects pickups, then checks exit collision. An exit collision enters the clearing animation immediately, **skipping that input's charge**. Rune clicks deduct 1 first; if already over the limit, you lose immediately without casting. Otherwise casting animates and its completed effect deducts another 1 (even if enlargement fails). Hints and blank clicks are free. There is no Undo.

**Level changes and memory**: `on_set_level` clears runes, cumulative charges, and both teleport indices, rebuilding the character/doors/pickups and spell list from the new level data. **Fireball direction `jdmucabyqar` and hint spell `ijhfdcamokt` are not reset**: the former starts downward; the latter is automatically overwritten only in levels 1–3 and cleared to `None` when casting. Thus levels 4–6 may retain a previous hint, even showing a teleport pattern unavailable in the current level. A hint does not mean an unlock. A single current frame also cannot reveal a hidden large-teleport index. The bar is quantized to 32 cells, so exact charges require action records. Only constructing a new game instance runs `__init__`.

**Engine verification and unused data**

- **Sensor-region clicks confirmed**: Official `Level.get_sprite_at()` tests layer, collidable, bbox/physical pixels without excluding invisible sprites. Separate reset tests at L2 `(22,47)`, `(28,50)`, `(38,63)` each toggled the corresponding rune cell. The old reconstructed engine's “blank regions do not count” does not describe official play.
- Level-data `rpjr: True` (levels 1–3) is **never read** by the code; its purpose is unknown.
- `crzdcq` (4×4, color 3) appears in fireball/collision checks, but **no level places it**, so it is never encountered.
- Each level has a large invisible, noncollidable color-1 image (L1–L6: `sprite-9`/`sprite-10`/`sprite-11`/`sprite-12`/`sprite-13`/`sprite-14`), never referenced by code, with no gameplay effect and unknown purpose. Other unreferenced, unplaced graphics include `sprite-19`, `sprite-16`, `sprite-18`, `sprite-15`, `sprite-20`, `sprite-17`.
- Four functions—`wxohoxzlfh` (light the next cell in order), `mutdsnbppd`, `tnqgbsvebp`, `oupzphkuwu`—are defined with **no call sites**, presumably remnants of a removed `ACTION5` design.
- Official `ARCBaseGame.try_move()`/`try_move_sprite()` were checked directly: attempt the move, gather collisions, then restore the original position if colliding. `Sprite.collides_with()` selects bbox/pixel testing by blocking mode. There is no need to infer behavior from dc22's same-named implementation.
