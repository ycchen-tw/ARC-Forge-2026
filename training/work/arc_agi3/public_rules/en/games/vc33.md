> The rules below were verified by measured runs of all 7 levels using `pixel/harness.py vc33` (cleared in 3/7/23/21/44/20/63 actions).
> Differences from the old static-inspection draft are marked **✱ Correction**.

**Screen structure**: The screen contains parallel **lanes**, separated by thin color-5 **walls** (thickness = that level's push increment).
Each lane contains a color-0 **piston**: **the end toward the push direction stays fixed** (against the screen boundary or a barrier),
while the other end (called the **end face** below) extends/retracts. Passengers (color-4 bodies + color-11/14/15 identity stripes) **stick to the outside of the end face**;
the passenger moves exactly as far as that face moves.

- ✱ Correction: Color 5 represents walls separating lanes, not “track lines”; these walls always extend inward from the screen boundary on the push side.
- ✱ Correction: Passengers do not “stand on a platform”; they are attached to the piston's **rear end face** (downward-push levels look as though they stand on top;
  rightward-push levels show them against the left side). **Passenger position is not an independent variable; it is the end face of its supporting piston**.

**Two button types (the only interactions)**

- **Extension/retraction buttons** (solid color-9 squares, ✱ **side length exactly equal to the push increment**): A button is **in the lane it will extend,
  immediately beside a wall**. Clicking shortens the piston **across the wall** by one increment and lengthens the piston on this side by one increment.
  The two lanes' passengers therefore move in opposite directions. Push direction and increment are level-specific
  (`Gravity`: right2 / left2 / down2 / down3 / right3 / left3 / up2).
  - **Current official-engine correction: A length-0 piston can regrow**. The donor must have length > 0, and the receiver must be below its limit;
    a receiver length of 0 does not prevent `np.vstack` from restoring pixels. In L1, three consecutive presses of the same button from the original scene yielded
    lengths `(4,18)→(2,20)→(0,22)`; one reverse press gave `(2,20)`. Thus “zero means permanent death / length disappears”
    was a conclusion from the old reconstructed engine and does not apply to official 0.9.3. Legal transitions in existing levels conserve length in push increments.
  - Extension is capped: usually at the **inner ends** of the two walls flanking the lane (whichever is farther along the push direction);
    see below for lanes containing barriers.
- **Swap bridges** (color-1 bars drawn in wall gaps): Effective only when **both neighboring piston end faces lie exactly at the bridge's leading edge**.
  Clicking exchanges passengers between the two lanes (passengers are centered in their destination lane, with unchanged position along the push axis;
  if one side is empty, this simply transfers the passenger).
  - ✱ Correction: Activation highlights (color 1 → color 12, with bracket hints) are **redrawn only after extension/retraction or a swap**.
    `on_set_level` does not redraw them, so **the color at level entry may be stale**. To judge availability,
    geometry (both end faces == bridge leading edge) is more reliable than color.
  - Clicking an unaligned bridge wastes one action.

**Barriers** (color 5, ✱ **perpendicular** to the lane walls, crossing one lane)

- ✱ Correction: A barrier **splits its lane into two independent pistons** (one anchored at the screen edge, one at the back of the barrier),
  rather than merely “limiting length.” Levels 6 and 7 contain this structure.
- Extension cap: An end face may reach the barrier's near edge, but **a piston currently carrying a passenger must stop 4 px early**,
  leaving room for the passenger. ✱ The sole exception is level 6: the game hardcodes **6 px** clearance when `Gravity == left 3`
  (apparently intended to check for a “large passenger,” but implemented as a direction check).

**Success conditions** (✱ Correction: the old draft's “deliver beside the marker” was too loose)

Each passenger must satisfy both:

1. Its lane **borders a wall bearing a same-color marker**—a lane on **either side** of that wall qualifies.
2. Its identity stripe and the wall marker **overlap exactly along the push axis** (the game compares sprite-origin coordinates for equality;
   since their lengths along that axis match, this is equivalent to “the two colored bands align,” and also to
   **supporting piston's end face == marker leading-edge coordinate**). This is strict equality; even one cell off fails, so parity must match.

All passengers must be in place simultaneously; judgment runs once at the end of each action.

**Other measured points**

- Every `ACTION6` consumes one action, **including empty clicks, unaligned bridge clicks, and buttons that cannot move a piston**.
  When click N exhausts a level's N-unit budget, **all passengers are checked first; only then does failure cause `lose`**. The last action can still clear.
  A swap bridge's pixel-by-pixel animation finishes inside the same action and costs no extra budget; judgment waits until the full animation ends.
- A passenger occupies only one piston, and bridges merely swap passengers, so each piston carries at most one passenger at any time.
- The 32×32 levels (1 and 2) use 2× camera zoom; the others use 1×. The 48×48 / 52×52 levels have color-4
  letterboxing, and color 4 is also the passenger body color—crop to the board before detecting passengers to avoid confusion.
